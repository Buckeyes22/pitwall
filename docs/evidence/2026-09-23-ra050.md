## Next reproduction strategy

The historical failure log has neither the randomized seed nor the request task's state at the
timeout. The test's readiness helper (`_wait_for_stream_ready`) now reports the request's state:
an early response or exception keeps its status, and a timeout reports the pending request's
stack. The next step is to keep those
diagnostics in CI and act on the first failure they capture. More seed sweeps would not add
evidence (the September 21 review says the same).
